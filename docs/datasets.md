# Dataset controls

All external datasets are tracked through DVC but are not committed to Git or
uploaded to Hugging Face. A dataset is downloaded only after its source,
license, intended purpose, and reviewer are recorded in the dataset registry.

Manga109 is an academic benchmark. Manga109-s may support commercial use of
machine-learning results but does not allow its comic images to become the
product catalog. Partner-licensed media is the only production catalog source.

`COMICS` and `Anime Face Dataset` are not precise enough to acquire safely.
They remain blocked until their exact upstream repository and license are
identified. Danbooru remains blocked for product ingestion because rights are
per image.
